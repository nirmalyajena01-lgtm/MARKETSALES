Cookie & Security Notice: Marketline uses essential HTTP cookies and secure browser storage strictly for session validation, CAPTCHA defense, and transaction idempotency. No third-party profiling or advertising trackers are deployed.

Terms of Service

1. System Usage & Access
Marketline POS is licensed strictly for authorized retail operations, checkout processing, and inventory management. Operator accounts are personal and non-transferable.

2. Financial Integrity & Reconciliation
All scanned sales, refunds, and price overrides are immutably logged with the operator's cryptographic signature and timestamp. Idempotency tokens guarantee that no sale is billed twice.

3. Security Responsibilities
Operators must safeguard their credentials and 2FA authentication tokens. Any suspected credential compromise must be immediately reported to store management.

Privacy & Security Policy

1. Data Minimization
Marketline collects only the operator credentials, role permissions, and transaction receipts necessary to execute checkout operations and regulatory accounting.

2. Cryptographic Storage
All operator passwords are salted and hashed using PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 with 600,000 iterations. Temporary two-factor authentication codes expire within 5 minutes and are never persisted in plaintext.

3. Confidential Cost Price Masking
Item cost prices are strictly restricted to managerial and financial analysis roles. Floor cashiers and inventory staff receive only retail pricing data.
